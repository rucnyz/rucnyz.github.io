I am currently an [Anthropic Fellow](https://www.anthropic.com/). Before that, I was an AI Research Scientist Intern at [Meta Superintelligence Lab](https://ai.meta.com/), working on post-training, where I contributed to [Muse Spark 1.2](https://developer.meta.com/ai/models/muse-spark/). I also have a side interest in inference system optimization.

I am currently pursuing my PhD in Computer Science at the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://www.cs.ucsb.edu/), advised by [Prof. Wenbo Guo](https://henrygwb.github.io/), working closely with [Prof. Bo Li](https://aisecure.github.io/) and [Prof. Dawn Song](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~dawnsong/). I received my bachelor's degree in Statistics from [Renmin University of China](http://www.ruc.edu.cn/en), where I was advised by [Prof. Hongteng Xu](https://hongtengxu.github.io/).

Previously, I was a founding engineer at [Virtue AI](https://www.virtueai.com/), leading large-scale post-training of cybersecurity-specialized LLMs.
